# webgl experiments

a shelf of things i wanted to touch before they existed. most of them are one gesture: drag, paint, flick, or wire.

[web-gl-experiments.vercel.app](https://web-gl-experiments.vercel.app)

## on the shelf

**01 grass.** a field of blades sitting in a wind simulation. move the cursor and the grass leans around your hand. sliders set how hard the wind blows and which way it comes from.

**03 lock.** a combination lock you spin by dragging. the dials have weight: they coast, slow down, and click into a digit.

**04 watercolor.** paint on wet paper. the brush, the ink, the water, and how far the edge is allowed to bleed are all on the panel.

**05 oil pastel.** the dry cousin of the watercolor. a stick with pressure, softness, blend, and the tooth of the paper underneath.

**06 flipbook.** a stack of frames redrawn in pencil. pick a source (one of them is lebron), set the stroke, and flick through the pages.

**08 breadboard.** a board you can actually build on. drop resistors, LEDs, logic chips, and an Arduino, run wires between them, and watch the circuit.

**09 design tiles.** the words *thisisneverthat*, treated like a physical lockup. stretch the letters, recolor them, and send a wave through the line.
